# Official-store game discovery

Required pages: home, list/search, category, detail, about/privacy/contact, 404. Optional editorial collections need their own real selection and route; do not add filler articles merely for page count.

Detail binds identity, real icon/screenshots, understandable summary, separate platform facts and official platform actions. It never implies a different browser game is the named mobile product. No APK, fake Download or unimplemented Play action.

Record source URL, region, timestamp and each platform's values. Unknown fields remain null/omitted. Store URLs must identify the intended app on apps.apple.com or play.google.com; a 200 redirect to a store home page is not product proof. Verify exact identifiers, not just host names.

Screenshot galleries preserve native ratios and offer keyboard/touch navigation, disabled limits and a correct counter. Editorial art is kept separate from official screenshots. Store links open clearly and safely. No fabricated rating, install count, price or popularity claim.
