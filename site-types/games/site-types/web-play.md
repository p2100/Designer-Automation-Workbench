# Browser game discovery

Required pages: home, list/search, category, detail, independent play, about/privacy/contact, 404. Genres may share the list family but need valid dedicated routes and recovery.

Detail identity and input/device judgment precede Play. Source touch claims and tested touch are visibly different. Include true controls and game language; unknown is not support. Use a clean runtime or an explicitly documented local adaptation. Do not carry source portal ads or analytics into the product.

Play preserves the entity and return destination. It has opening, running, stalled/error, retry, help/pause guidance and exit/return. Canvas orientation and safe space must suit the actual game. Do not invent universal WASD, shooting, joystick or swipe controls. Do not add a PC fullscreen button or a separate Back to detail button; keep normal exit/recovery and browser navigation usable. Recent games are browser-local, truthful and clearable.

Runtime acceptance observes boot/game identity, starts, source-specific input, pause/resume, ordinary restart/failure and return. Advertising-free adaptations also test reward/failed-run paths and actual network requests. Ordinary pointer, synthetic touch and real hardware are separate evidence types.
