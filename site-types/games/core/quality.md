# Shared craft and truth contract

The current [owner games contract](owner-games-rules.md) and [itemized checks](owner-requirements.json) take precedence for the regions they cover. A common home grid or explicitly selected reference skeleton is authorized; a historical reference does not fix every future site's inner layout. Respect the latest approved images and authorized free composition. Do not apply Coupon-specific business or dimensions to Games.

## Truth and purpose

Use real source-linked games. Keep title, image, description, runtime and store destination attached to one entity. Category and editorial selection are identified as editorial, not invented popularity or rankings. Platform/region/date fields are independent; null is unknown. Do not transfer iOS size, age or rating to Android.

Web-play records source claims separately from executed verification: keyboard, pointer, synthetic touch, real device, orientation, language and embedding are different observations. Do not label all games mobile-ready because they fit a phone. Store-referral has no fake Play, APK or intermediary download.

Original source is immutable. Hash adaptations, their expected matches and reasons. Advertising-free includes runtime scripts and reward offers; disabling a bridge must not fake a successful reward. Audit old analytics too. A successful HTTP response is not game execution. Test loading, failure, retry, pause/help, exit and return.

## Layout and legibility

Each module has a job and first focus. Choose image role, content grouping, card capacity, spacing and action placement from that job. On the same site, same-purpose ordinary cards retain their reading axis, fields and capacity through the last row. Different modules may use different families; avoid accidental one-off card shapes.

Use readable body/game names around 16px, secondary facts around 14px, comfortable line height and actions at least 44×44 CSS px. These are initial budgets: inspect actual glyphs, long titles, wrapping and controls at 320px. A field cannot be hidden or shrunk to rescue an unsuitable column count. Summaries may have an explicit clamp with the complete content reachable. Main titles, essential device conditions and primary actions remain understandable.

Ordinary/recommended game names, including search results, stay one line with ellipsis when necessary. Preserve full DOM/accessibility and full main identity/summary/body. Caption padding and capacity are consistent within each brand's family. A report about one instance triggers a bounded sweep of all affected sibling contexts, including lower recommendations and client-rendered results; do not wait for separate owner reports. Check primary action labels at intermediate widths as well as phones.

Images keep their declared role/proportions. Reserve space before loading; verify HTML width/height attributes do not defeat CSS aspect-ratio. For product screenshots use contain where necessary, never distort portrait media into a landscape crop. A browser screenshot is evidence, not a source image replacement. Generated topic art is explicitly editorial.

For a game stage combining aspect-ratio and minimum height, verify its actual width remains constrained to the parent at narrow sizes. Fix the component's sizing, not the symptom with root overflow masking.

Measure inner padding, item gaps, heading-to-module and module-to-module spacing. Look at the complete page for needless holes and crowding; do not make unrelated panels equally tall merely to fill a grid. Compare image subjects with the complete text group, not only box edges. Read continuous lists, short/long items and last rows.

Design mobile grouping deliberately: adjust columns, field order and action ownership. Keep genre names and icons compact, preserve useful filters and avoid tiny ordinary four-column text. Local horizontal navigation can scroll with a discoverable affordance; the page itself must not drift horizontally. Check dropdowns, text and arrows inside their containers.

## Actions and states

Semantic anchors navigate; buttons change state. Labels match real destinations. No pretend form success, empty links or buttons with no behavior. Galleries have keyboard controls, position state and natural touch scrolling. Menus have correct expanded state and Escape behavior. Focus is visible and not clipped.

Hover/focus/pressed feedback must not move neighbors or change text geometry. Reserve image and result space. Check first load, first scroll, font load, search/filter repaint, empty results and reduced motion. Ordinary cards must remain correct after client rendering, not just in the first HTML.

## Distinction and review

When the user explicitly requests matching a reference layout, that lock owns the page's module order, columns, media/action positions and responsive regrouping. Do not invent a different reading structure to satisfy the general distinction check below. Preserve the chosen brands through typography, color roles, iconography and component treatment; compare the requested layout against the reference at equal widths. Record authorized omissions such as ads, video, fake actions or unsupported factual fields instead of recreating their holes or numbers. This exception applies only to the user-locked pages, not unrelated site pages.

Compare same-purpose pages and modules side by side at the same desktop and phone widths. Ignore colors, corners, shadows and decorative buttons: grouping, reading order, image/text relationships or action placement should still explain a useful distinction. A special hero does not exempt normal cards, categories, related games or the play shell. Do not force irrelevant novelty into familiar task controls.

The independent reviewer first observes the real candidate and records its first focus/order/rhythm, then reads the author's plan. Review every declared family and source-linked sample, plus any actual family the plan missed. Missing coverage is not-run. P0/P1/P2 layout, identity, destination, behavior or major similarity issues require repair before final handoff. Evidence from an earlier candidate is not silently upgraded after a rebuild.

Tools can verify files and assertions; they cannot certify originality, beauty, true independence or a game being fun. Bind review evidence to candidate hashes. Report execution limits plainly. User approval of a direction is different from acceptance of finished pages.

The latest [owner standards](owner-standards-20261027.md) (from a core file: owner-standards-20261027.md) apply in every author/review phase. They supersede older homepage category, inner-width, fullscreen and surface-palette directions. Use the portable inspector and actual browser/Image capabilities; preserve feedback and distinguish not-run from a pass.
