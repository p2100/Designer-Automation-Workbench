# Home

Read core/owner-standards-20261027.md and owner-requirements.json. The homepage fills the viewport with 20–40px side gutters and prefers square game icon/artwork frames. Game information appears on hover/focus and deliberate click/tap with usable phone disclosure/navigation. Accessible full names remain available. Names when displayed retain the compact-name rules.

The owner's direct clarification on 30 October 2026 makes these three homepage modules required for new sites and authorized homepage maintenance:

- **Game grid:** the core game-led content, with deliberate density, large/small card composition and usable phone capacity.
- **Recently viewed:** actual game visits with matching entity links and a working clear action. Handle first visit and the cleared state honestly; do not substitute recommendations or invented browsing history. Keep a compact, intentional empty state rather than silently omitting this required module.
- **Brand introduction:** a dedicated, concise introduction describing this site's real identity, purpose and available play/store capabilities. Its layout and wording belong to this brand. Do not copy Playaro's colors, Logo arrangement, text or dimensions, invent claims, or let a large introduction displace the game-led opening.

Do not add homepage category modules, category sections or genre browsing blocks. Older lower-home category requirements are superseded. Existing actual category/list routes are maintained separately with real games/counts. Additional useful discovery interactions remain optional and brand-owned. The required brand introduction is substantive content in its own module; it does not permit decorative subtitle copy under every module heading. Keep finite content height and stable last rows; do not fill space with duplicates or twelve tiny ordinary cards.

A hover button is optional design, not a universal component. Each site's information/action presentation and game-themed background are deliberate and distinct. Textures, geometry and patterns have meaningful game logic, restrained attention and coherent full-page transitions without seams. Inspect the complete canvas from top to footer on PC/phone.

Bind real game identities and generated editorial icon provenance separately from official screenshots. Check ordinary/featured groups, long labels, meaningful first/middle/last rows, phone tap feedback and repaint states. Do not claim popularity or unsupported input capability.
