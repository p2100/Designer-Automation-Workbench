Game information appears on hover/focus and deliberate click/tap with usable phone disclosure/navigation. Accessible full names remain available. Names when displayed retain the compact-name rules.

Do not add homepage category modules, category sections or genre browsing blocks. Older lower-home category requirements are superseded. Existing actual category/list routes are maintained separately with real games/counts. Useful authorized discovery interactions can remain without category modules or introductory subtitle copy. Keep finite content height and stable last rows; do not fill space with duplicates or twelve tiny ordinary cards.

A hover button is optional design, not a universal component. Each site's information/action presentation and game-themed background are deliberate and distinct. Textures, geometry and patterns have meaningful game logic, restrained attention and coherent full-page transitions without seams. Inspect the complete canvas from top to footer on PC/phone.

Bind real game identities and generated editorial icon provenance separately from official screenshots. Check ordinary/featured groups, long labels, meaningful first/middle/last rows, phone tap feedback and repaint states. Do not claim popularity or unsupported input capability.
